The Weekly Playbook

Follow this repeatable routine from Sunday through Tuesday to make focused decisions for the next matchup.

  1. Check every starter's injury status and availability.
  2. Compare the upcoming opponents for close lineup decisions.
  3. Review the waiver wire for players who fill a roster need.
  4. Revisit your lineup before the first game begins.
  5. Save one short note about what worked for next week.
